# Startup card photos

Put the pictures you want on the startup card in this folder (`startup/`),
then list their file names below, one per line. They play in this order,
one at a time. Portrait and landscape both work.

Lines starting with # are ignored. Supported: jpg, jpeg, png, webp, gif, avif.
If no listed file loads, the card falls back to photos from your library.
